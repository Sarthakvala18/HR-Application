It leaks money

A Zoom license not removed before the billing cycle gets billed again. License removal is buried behind plan management and a CVV prompt, so it slips.

It leaks access

Offboarding is "reverse everything, by hand." Until someone finishes the list, a departed employee still holds email, CRM, passwords, and client recordings.

It lives in heads

The process exists as one Word doc and the memory of whoever did it last. Every step names a person: named individuals.

03

Three layers, one source of truth.

Layer 1 · RecordZoho People
The employee ledger: profiles, departments, managers, join and exit dates, leave balances, the holiday calendar. Adding a hire here is the one action that triggers everything else.
Layer 2 · Enginen8n workflows
Five workflows: Onboard, Offboard, Leaves, Letters, License-Guard. They call the Google, Zoho, Zoom, Slack, and Bitwarden APIs in the right order, pause at human approval gates, and log every step.
Layer 3 · CoworkerClaude
The conversational front door. "Onboard Rahul, sales, starts Aug 1, reports to Meera" validates the request, drafts the letter, fires the workflow, and reports progress. Holds no destructive credentials; n8n owns all keys.
04

Onboarding: one form in, a teammate out.

Solid dots are human approval gates. Money never moves without a named person clicking yes; everything mechanical happens on its own.

HR fills one form 2 minutes

Name, personal email, department, role template, manager, start date. Role templates carry everything downstream: groups, channels, licenses, vault collections.

Letter drafted & signed

Claude drafts the appointment letter with the R&R section filled from the role template. HR reviews, Zoho Sign sends it. Freelancers get the NDA instead, and nothing provisions until it is signed.

License check Human gate

Free seats counted across Google, Zoom, Zoho. If short, a Slack approval card goes to the licence approver, the licence approver, or the HR lead with the exact cost. The workflow waits for the click.

Accounts cascade

Google account with groups and drives, Zoho One apps, Desk agent with forwarding, Zoom with the correct timezone, Bitwarden collections. Slack invite arrives as a prefilled one-click task (Pro plan keeps invites manual, everything else automates).

Day-one welcome

Welcome post in #champions, manager receives the day-one checklist, every completed step ticked in Zoho People automatically. Full audit trail of who got what.

05

Offboarding: access dies in minutes, data lives on.

Order is the whole game. Access first, data second, letters last. For terminations the first three steps run within minutes of the trigger.

Suspend Google Instant

Password reset, every session signed out, all OAuth tokens revoked. Because Google is the identity backbone, this alone cuts most access company-wide.

Vault & apps revoked

Bitwarden membership removed and a rotation list generated for every shared credential they could read. Zoho deactivated with open Desk tickets reassigned. Zoom deactivated with meetings and recordings transferred.

License guard Stops the leak

Freed Zoom licenses are flagged for reuse by the next hire. If still unused three days before the billing cycle, a removal reminder fires. No more paying for ghosts.

Data lands where it should

Drive and mail migrate by the department matrix below. After a 24-hour verification, the user is deleted and the old address becomes an alias so no client email ever bounces.

Letters & audit

Relieving and experience letters drafted from our templates, approved by HR, sent from hr@example.com. The exit checklist in Zoho People shows who triggered what, and when.
