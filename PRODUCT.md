# PRODUCT.md — HR Automation Pitch

## Product purpose
A single-file HTML pitch page presenting the HR onboarding/offboarding automation plan (see PLAN.md) to Coach Foundation's founders for approval. It must persuade in one screen-share: the pain is real, the fix uses only tools already paid for, and the ask is small.

## Register
brand

## Users
- Primary: Coach Foundation founders/leadership (Sai and core team), viewing over a Zoom screen-share or a shared link on a laptop, daytime, bright room.
- Secondary: HR/ops team (the HR lead, the HR lead, the HR lead) who will recognize their own daily manual work in it.

## Brand & tone
Coach Foundation is a coaching-industry education business: warm, human, credible, operationally serious. The pitch should feel like an internal strategy memo art-directed by a magazine: editorial, confident, zero SaaS-vendor gloss.

## Anti-references
- Dark-mode dev-tool aesthetic (this is an HR/people document)
- Generic SaaS landing page (gradient blobs, hero-metric cards, icon card grids)
- Corporate deck clip-art

## Strategic principles
- Recognition beats abstraction: recreate the actual Slack #champions posts they see every day.
- Money and security arguments lead; tech architecture supports.
- Every claim traceable to the real SOP (HR Tasks.docx) and observed channel history.
- $0-new-software is the headline economic fact.
